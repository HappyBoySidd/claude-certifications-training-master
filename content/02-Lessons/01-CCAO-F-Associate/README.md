# CCAO-F lessons (Claude Certified Associate: Foundations)

Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

One lesson per official domain, plus L00 orientation. Each lesson has the big idea, what to know,
a worked example, traps, a hands-on "Try it" and a "Done when" checklist. L01–L07 each end with a
practice drill at an 80% pass bar.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L00 | [Orientation, plus a cold baseline mock](L00-orientation.md) | none | 30 min | practice tool |

| L02 | [Output Evaluation and Validation](L02-output-evaluation-and-validation.md) | **21%** | 90 min | [Lesson 2 practice set](https://git.soma.salesforce.com/pages/akumthekar/claude-certifications-training/practice/index.html) |

**Order:** L00 first. Then take your weakest domains from the baseline first, but always do L02,
because it's a fifth of the exam. After L07, go to the
[CCAO-F simulations](../../04-Simulations/01-CCAO-F-Associate/README.md).
