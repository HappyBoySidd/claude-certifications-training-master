Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

Take these in the order below, **on different days**. Exam-day conditions: one sitting, timed, no
notes, no documentation, no Claude.

**What counts toward being ready:** only scores on questions you haven't seen before. Repeat runs
are for pacing and timing, because scores rise from memory faster than skill. The full readiness
bar is in [Before you book](../../01-Curriculum/01-Associate-Track/CCAO-F.md#before-you-book).

## After each run

1. In the tool, choose **Fix my mistakes** to redo only the questions you got wrong.
2. For any domain below 70%, reread that lesson's "Traps", then drill that domain in the tool.
3. Note the date, run, score, the two weakest domains, and what you'll do next.

**Note:** most of the practice tool's questions are community-written, not Anthropic's. None
are real exam questions. It's good for volume, but trust the official guide wherever the two disagree.
