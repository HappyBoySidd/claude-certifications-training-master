# CCAR-F simulations

**Learn before rehearsing:** [Choose a coached application](../../02-Lessons/02-CCAR-F-Architect-Foundations/architecture-studio/README.md). The six family simulations below are independent rehearsals, not the total scenario inventory. Answer the technical decisions before opening the coach key. After checking, use the local layered replay to explain the failure and repair in ordinary words, then return to the same objective for targeted practice.


The saved July 2026 guide describes four sampled scenarios from six named families. These six family simulations rehearse that baseline; they are not an exhaustive catalog of possible application contexts. Start with the [full learning journey](../../02-Lessons/02-CCAR-F-Architect-Foundations/learning-journey.md) and its eight coached applications if the architecture is unfamiliar.

**On your own:** write your answers to a scenario's decisions, then open its coach key.
Each scenario has 10 or 11 decisions, and every answer in the key cites an official task
statement. In the keys, a tag like **D1 1.4** means Domain 1, task statement 1.4 of the official
exam guide. After the key, each scenario has a **Round 2: twists** section: the same scenario with
one thing changed, so you practise how the right answer moves.

**Readiness check:** Use unseen scenario questions, explain your decisions and alternatives, and revisit errors after a delay. Local percentages and mock results are study signals; this site's mock scores do not predict an official scaled score or guarantee a pass.

[Scenario learning studio](../../02-Lessons/02-CCAR-F-Architect-Foundations/scenario-studio.md) connects 30 original decision variants, industry transfer, drawings and targeted questions. Practise both the six blueprint families and changed constraints.
