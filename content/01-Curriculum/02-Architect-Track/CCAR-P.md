# Architect track, level 2 · CCAR-P Claude Certified Architect: Professional

**Exam:** 63 questions · $175 list · 7 domains · check the time and pass mark in the [official guide](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications)
**Study time:** 15–20 hours, about 2 weeks at 10 hours a week
**What others learned:** [Insights](../../11-Lessons-Learned/INSIGHTS.md#ccar-p-architect-professional)

**Prerequisite:** [CCAR-F](CCAR-F.md) is recommended, not required. In July 2026 a colleague
read an Anthropic email as saying Foundations isn't required.

## Week plan

| Week | Focus | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Integration (19%), Solution design (17%), Evaluation and testing (16%) | Lessons L00–L03, then SIM-02 and SIM-03. Courses: MCP Advanced Topics, plus the cloud course for your customers' platform. Lab 05: a test set, graded scoring and a regression check for your CCAR-F extraction lab. Amey's flashcards suggest routing human review by confidence and consequence; practice that too. |
| 2 | Governance (14%), Stakeholders and lifecycle (14%), Models and context (13%) | Lessons L04–L07, then SIM-04 and SIM-05. This is your day job. Map one real engagement to the blueprint: RACI, rollout, change management, cost governance. Amey Thakur's mocks [1](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/architect-professional/mock-exam-1.md), [2](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/architect-professional/mock-exam-2.md) and [3](https://github.com/Amey-Thakur/CLAUDE-CERTIFICATIONS/blob/main/architect-professional/mock-exam-3.md) (Tier 2, MIT licence), plus the practice tool. **Sit the exam.** |
