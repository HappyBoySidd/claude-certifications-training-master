# Associate track · CCAO-F Claude Certified Associate: Foundations

Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

**Exam:** 60 questions in 120 minutes · pass mark 720 on a 100–1,000 scale · $99 · valid 12 months · 7 domains
**Study time:** about 18 hours over about 2 weeks, in 8 sessions (mocks included; see the week plan)
**Start here:** [L00 orientation](../../02-Lessons/01-CCAO-F-Associate/L00-orientation.md) (30 min), then the cold baseline mock (30 min).
**What others learned:** [Insights](../../11-Lessons-Learned/INSIGHTS.md#ccao-f-associate)

**Who it's for.** In the exam guide's own words (official wording, exam guide v1.0, section 1 and section 3):
"It is intended for professionals who use Claude as a productivity tool in roles such as operations,
marketing, project management, education, and communications." "The audience includes both internal
staff who maintain and optimize ongoing AI-enabled workflows and external consultants who support
implementation, use-case identification, and process redesign." It is not for software developers who
build against APIs or design agentic systems.

**For partners:** it doesn't count toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility (Partner FAQ).

## Before you book

**Book a date early so you commit, then confirm you're ready before the reschedule window closes.**
The guide lets you cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment; changes inside 24
hours forfeit the fee. Play it safe and decide 48 hours out (Pearson's own page says 48).

**Booking steps**

1. Sign in to the Partner Academy with your **Salesforce email**, directly, not "Sign in with
   Google" (field reports, F).
2. Make sure your registration name matches your government photo ID **exactly**. To fix a name,
   email certifications-support@anthropic.com **before** you schedule (exam guide section 11, Tier 1).
3. Check the price at checkout. If you'll be charged, get expense or training pre-approval
   **before** you pay (field reports, F).
4. Testing online? Run the OnVUE system test on the **same laptop** a few days ahead. Company-managed
   laptops have blocked it for some people (contested, F). If the test fails, book a Pearson test
   center.

**Ready to sit when all three are true** (this is the one readiness bar for CCAO-F; other pages link
here. It's our bar, not Anthropic's; you can set a stricter one):

1. **Scenario simulations:** SIM-02 to SIM-05 each at 7 of 8 or better, and SIM-06 at 8 of 10 or
   better, on your **first** run of each.
2. **Two timed mocks at 850 or more, with no domain under 70%, on questions you haven't seen
   before.** Repeat runs are for pacing and don't count, because scores rise from memory. If a mock
   gives only a raw percentage, read 85% as 850 (our rough equivalent, not Anthropic's).
   - On the site, the unseen questions are Amey Thakur's mocks 2 and 3 (mock 1 is your cold
     baseline). They're 15 questions each, so take them as one 30-question, 60-minute check and
     count it once. That's only one unseen check on the site today; a bigger public question bank
     is planned for the second.
   - 
3. **Official practice exam: none exists right now.** Anthropic's Partner FAQ says the practice
   exam on the old platform "was retired in the move to Pearson" and points to the guide's sample
   questions instead (Tier 1). So this line is just: get all 3 sample questions in guide section 8 right
   and be able to say why each wrong option is wrong.

Some study notes say to aim for 900 on practice mocks. Treat that as a stretch target; the bar above
is the one we use.

## Week plan (about 18 hours, about 2 weeks)

Each lesson is followed by its drill (about 15 minutes). Mocks get their own sessions and never go
on exam day.

| Session | Time | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 h | L00 (30 min). Cold baseline: Amey mock 1, timed (30 min). Log the score. Read the guide's blueprint table (30 min). |
| 2 | 2.5 h | L01 and L03, each with its drill |
| 3 | 2 h | L02 (90 min), then its drill twice |
| 4 | 2.5 h | L06 and L04, each with its drill. Add the [prep-path modules](../00-Overview.md#3-courses-shared-across-exams-take-each-once) for any domain that's still weak. |
| 5 | 2 h | L05 and L07, each with its drill |
| 6 | 2 h | Lab 01 (60–90 min): set up a real Project and mark 5 outputs pass, revise or fail. Then SIM-02. |
| 7 | 2.5 h | SIM-03, SIM-04, SIM-05 and SIM-06. Reread the traps for every miss. Drill your weakest domain, then "Fix my mistakes". |
| 8 | 2 h | Two timed mocks on unseen questions (see SIM-01). Reread the traps in each weak lesson. Check the bar in "Before you book". |

Sit the exam on a later day, rested.
