# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this study site better. The short version: **open an issue, not a pull
request.**

## How to help

Pick a form under **Issues → New issue**:

| Form | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Report a problem** | A wrong fact, a broken link, a confusing page or a design issue. The link at the bottom of every page fills in the page for you. |
| **Request content** | A topic, lab, simulation, clearer explanation or site feature you'd like. |
| **Exam debrief** | How your exam went: topics, pacing and prep advice. Never actual questions. |

Prefer Slack? Message the site owner and they'll send you the right form.

## Why no pull requests

The pages in `docs/` are generated by `site/build.py` from a source folder that isn't in this
repository. Anything edited here directly would be overwritten on the next build. So changes come
in as issues and are copied into the source by hand. If you send a pull request anyway, it's read
as a proposal and closed once the change lands, with credit if you want it.

## What happens to your issue

1. It's triaged about once a week and gets a reply and a label.
2. Accepted issues go on the work list, and the issue says so.
3. The change passes the site's review steps (sources, scope, accuracy, teaching, learner check
   and the quality gate).
4. The issue closes when the change is live, and it's listed in `CHANGELOG.md` and on the
   **What's new** page. You're credited only if you agree.

## Ground rules

- No real exam questions or answers, ever. The exams come with a confidentiality agreement.
- Claude and Anthropic only.
- Facts need a source, Anthropic's own docs and exam guides first.
- Only share material you're allowed to share.
- Names stay private unless you choose to be credited.

Design changes follow the site's **Design style guide** page.
