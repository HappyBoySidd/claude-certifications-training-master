Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what colleagues ask most. If your question isn't here, ask it through the Contribute page.

About the site

Is this an official Anthropic site?

No. It's an internal study site made by colleagues. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Facts come from Anthropic's own material first, and the official exam guide always wins (source tiers).

Which exams does it cover?

Anthropic's four Claude certifications: CCAO-F Associate, CCAR-F Architect Foundations, CCDV-F Developer Foundations and CCAR-P Architect Professional. Each has its own study plan. The site covers Claude and Anthropic only, not other companies' products or certifications.

I can't open the site.

It sits behind the Git Soma login. Sign in to Git Soma with your Salesforce account first, then open the link again.

Does the site track me?

No. There are no cookies, no analytics and no calls to other sites. Your theme and checklist ticks are saved only in your own browser.

Studying

Where should I start?

It depends on your role. The home page has a path chooser, and the curriculum explains the three paths: people who don't write code start with CCAO-F; architects and developers start with CCAR-F.

How long does it take to prepare?

Each exam page gives a week plan and study hours. For CCAR-F, colleagues who passed reported about 20 to 30 hours (Insights; field reports, not official).

How do I book?

If you want the fee paid back, first get pre-approval in Forma and your manager's OK (see below). Then book on Anthropic's Partner Academy with your Salesforce email. Type the email in yourself rather than using "Sign in with Google": one colleague who used Google sign-in was charged the full price. Each exam page, for example CCAR-F, has a "Before you book" checklist with the format, time and pass mark from the official exam guide.

What does it cost?

Anthropic's list prices (its certification pages, for example CCAR-F, checked 2026-09-28):

ExamList price
CCAO-F Associate$99 USD
CCAR-F Architect Foundations$125 USD
CCDV-F Developer Foundations$125 USD
CCAR-P Architect Professional$175 USD

Some colleagues saw the exam as free, or got a credit, after registering with their Salesforce email. Others were charged the full price with no discount. So check the price at checkout before you pay (Insights; field reports).

Can I get the fee paid back?

Colleagues report that the fee is covered by Salesforce's Global Education Reimbursement Program in Forma. Its rules, from BaseCamp (internal policy, so check the current version there):

  1. Before you book, ask for pre-approval in Forma and get your manager's OK. Without it, the claim may be refused.
  2. Pay with your own card, not a company card.
  3. Claim within 6 months of taking the exam (the policy says "of completion").

Exam fees, renewal fees and prep courses all count, up to a yearly limit ($5,250 USD, or your country's limit). If no discount shows at checkout, pay and claim the same way.

How do I use the simulations?

Read the scene, answer the ten decisions on paper, then open the coach key. Or paste the scene and decisions into Claude and ask it to quiz you one decision at a time. See Simulations.

Can I study with Claude?

Yes. Paste any lesson into Claude and ask it to teach you in short parts and check your understanding. The About page has prompts you can copy.

Questions and practice

Does the site have real exam questions?

No, and it never will. Candidates sign a confidentiality agreement (why). Everything here is our own material, written from Anthropic's public exam guides and docs (Resources).

Is there a practice question bank?

Yes. The practice tool is one file you download and open in your browser, even offline. It has timed mocks, domain drills and a "Fix my mistakes" mode. It holds Anthropic's own sample questions (Tier 1) and 319 questions from Amey Thakur's open-source repo (Tier 2, MIT licence), which we spot-checked. None are real exam questions, so trust the official guide where they disagree. It sits in the repo, not on these pages, because most of it was written by someone else.

Someone offered me an "exam dump". Should I use it?

No. It breaks the exam agreement, and community answers are often wrong. Understand why an answer is right, using Anthropic's docs.

Helping out

I found a mistake. What do I do?

Use the Report a problem on this page link at the bottom of the page with the mistake. It opens a short form with the page already filled in. For a wrong fact, add the link you're going by.

Can I suggest a topic, a lab or a better explanation?

Yes, please. See Contribute.

I passed. Can I share how it went?

Yes, that helps the most. Use the exam debrief form on the Contribute page. Share topics, pacing and prep advice, never actual questions. You're kept anonymous unless you say otherwise.

Can I edit the site myself?

Not directly. The pages are generated from a source folder, so direct edits would be overwritten on the next build. Send your change through the Contribute page and it'll be folded in, with credit if you want it.

How do I know what changed?

Every update is listed on What's new, and the home page shows the latest one. Updates that add pages or exercises, or change a fact or an answer, are also announced in the study cohort's Slack channel.
