Curriculum overview: three tracks, four exams

Anthropic's certifications fall into three tracks. Each exam has its own curriculum file, so plans, lessons and progress never mix. This page covers only what applies across exams: the order, the shared method and the shared courses.

Why CCAR-F comes before CCAR-P: Anthropic's advice to the cohort, relayed by a colleague, is that neither is a prerequisite for the other. Our call: CCAR-P covers the same agent, tool and MCP topics at a harder level, so CCAR-F is the natural first step.

About 55–70 hours for all four. That's 8 weeks at about 10 hours a week, or about 14 weeks at 5. If you only need Claude Partner Network tier credit, skip CCAO-F: Associate doesn't count toward tiers. Background is in lessons learned.

2. The same six steps for every exam

  1. Book first, at the end of the planned window. Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before. Use the legal name on your photo ID.
  2. Cold baseline: one timed mock before studying. Skip any domain above 85%.
  3. Blueprint self-check: mark every objective yes, shaky or no.
  4. Lessons for the shaky and no domains, each followed by its practice session.
  5. One hands-on lab from Labs.
  6. Simulations, then mocks.

Ready to book when: two timed mocks in a row at 850 or more, and no domain under 70%. Anthropic's official practice exam was retired in the move to Pearson (Partner Academy certification FAQ, Tier 1). The only official practice items now are the exam guide's sample questions and the Anthropic Academy course quizzes, so use them as a final check, not a score bar.

4. Rules that save time

  • Study by weight. A 27% domain deserves about 3 times the hours of a 10% one.
  • Mocks beat reading. Four colleagues who passed all said so.
  • Pace yourself: 2 minutes a question. Flag the hard ones; guessing isn't penalized.
  • If you fail: you wait 14 days. Use them on your weakest domains from the score report.
  • Renewals: each credential lasts 12 months. Finishing all four in about 8 weeks keeps the renewals together.
