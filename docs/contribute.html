Contribute

This site gets better when people who use it say what's wrong or missing. Everything comes in through a short form on the Git Soma repository, so nothing gets lost.

Three ways to help

You want to…UseWhat happens next
Report a mistake, a broken link or a confusing pageReport a problem, or the link at the bottom of any pageChecked and fixed in the next update. Wrong facts are checked against Anthropic's docs first
Suggest a topic, lab, simulation or better explanationRequest contentAdded to the list and scheduled. Some ideas wait a few weeks
Share how your exam wentExam debriefFolded into Insights, anonymously unless you say otherwise

You need to be signed in to Git Soma to open a form. Prefer Slack? Message the site owner (shown on the repository page), and they'll send you the right form.

What happens after you send a form

  1. Read and sorted, about once a week: every issue gets a reply.
  2. Planned: accepted issues go on the site's work list, and the issue says so. Fixes usually ship within two weeks; bigger ideas can take longer.
  3. Fixed and checked: every change passes the site's review steps (how this site is built).
  4. Shipped: the issue closes when the change is live, and it's listed on What's new. You're credited only if you agree.

Declined ideas get a short reason. Nothing is closed silently.

The ground rules

  • No real exam questions or answers, ever. The exams come with a confidentiality agreement.
  • Claude and Anthropic only. Other companies' products and certifications are out of scope.
  • Facts need a source. Anthropic's own docs and exam guides come first (source tiers).
  • Only share what you're allowed to share. Don't paste someone else's course or question set without their permission.
  • Names stay private unless you choose to be credited.

Why there are no pull requests

The pages are generated from a source folder that isn't in the repository. A direct edit to the repository would be overwritten on the next build, so changes come in as issues and are copied in by hand. Design changes follow the design style guide.
