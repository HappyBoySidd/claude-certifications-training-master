v0.10 — 2026-10-09 — CCAR-F scenario walkthroughs

  • New: six scenario walkthroughs for CCAR-F. One page for each official exam scenario. Each page shows the whole job as one flowchart, then goes step by step: what you decide, the design rule, and what can go wrong (the exact signal or error code, and what the design does about it). Each page ends with 10 what-if questions with answers. The questions are written by the project. They are practice, not real exam questions. Find them under "Scenario walkthroughs" in the CCAR-F sidebar and at the top of the CCAR-F lessons page.
  • Scenario map: the walkthroughs page now has one card per scenario. Each card gives the domains, a one-line "remember it as", about 45 minutes of study time, and links to the walkthrough, its what-ifs, its simulation and the objectives it covers.
  • Linked study path: each CCAR-F scenario simulation now starts with "Study the walkthrough first". Each objective page lists the scenarios it shows up in. On the walkthrough pages, every [Guide N.N] tag links to that objective's page.
  • Big charts: each walkthrough chart scrolls in its own box. An "Open chart full size" button shows it full screen; press Esc or Close to go back.
  • In real life (beyond the exam): each walkthrough ends with a collapsed section on how the design works at work. It is closed by default and labelled "not exam rules". On exam day, the exam answer wins.
  • Tidier objective pages (CCAR-F 3.1–5.6): the top of each page now uses the same header as objectives 1.1–2.5: Exam, Domain, Time, Prerequisites and "Continue from", then the official task statement in the exam guide's own words. The lesson content, facts and answers did not change.
  • How we build: a short note on where project plans are tracked.
  • Not in this release: the downloadable practice tool is unchanged and stays at v0.9. Its next update comes in a later release.
