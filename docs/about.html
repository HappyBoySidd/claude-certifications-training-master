About this site

A study site for colleagues preparing for Anthropic's four Claude certifications. It covers Claude and Anthropic only.

What's here

  • A plan for each exam, with a checklist that remembers your ticks in this browser.
  • One lesson per exam domain, written from the official exam guide and Anthropic's docs.
  • Scenario simulations: eight to ten decisions each, with a coach key to open when you're done.
  • For CCAR-F: deep practice by domain, decision charts and sequence diagrams, objective pages, flashcards and hard drills.
  • Five hands-on labs, and what colleagues who passed said. Names are withheld until each person agrees.

What's not here, on purpose

  • No real exam questions. Candidates sign a confidentiality agreement, and we respect it.
  • No copied question banks. Practice sets by other people stay with their authors; see Resources.
  • No personal scores, no tracking, no cookies and no calls to other sites.

Third-party code

The diagrams are drawn in your browser by Mermaid 12.1.0 (MIT licence). A copy ships with the site, with its licence, so nothing is loaded from outside.

Studying with Claude

Paste any lesson into Claude and say "Teach me this in short parts and check my understanding." For a simulation, say "Run this simulation: one decision at a time, four options, no hints, and score me at the end."

Found a problem, or have an idea?

Use the Report a problem on this page link at the bottom of any page, or see Contribute for ideas, content requests and exam debriefs. Every defect gets a root cause and a check that stops it coming back. Common questions are in the FAQ, and each release is listed in What's new.
