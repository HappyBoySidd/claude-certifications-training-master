CCAR-P SIM-02 — Enterprise Knowledge Assistant (RAG)

How to use this: read the scene, then work through the decisions on paper, or paste the scene and the decisions list (not the answer key) into Claude and ask it to run the simulation one decision at a time with no hints. Open the coach key only when you've finished.

Primary domains: 3 Integration, 2 Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering, 4 Evaluation, Testing & Optimization · Decisions: 10 · Time: 20 min

Scene (original, written from the official objectives)

You're the architect for an internal knowledge assistant at a global engineering firm. It answers employee questions from a large mix of policy PDFs, project specs, wiki pages and rate-card spreadsheets, and it must cite its sources. Some people ask broad policy questions; others paste exact part codes. The index refreshes weekly, and some HR documents are confidential. The sponsor wants answers that are accurate, current and permission-aware.

The decisions (answer these before you open the key)

  1. Fixed-size chunks cut policy clauses in half
  2. The sponsor wants the most capable model at maximum effort for every question, but most questions are simple lookups
  3. Exact part codes like "PX-4471" return the wrong documents
  4. A developer proposes pasting every policy into one huge prompt on each request, "because the context window is big enough"
  5. After the weekly refresh the assistant cites a superseded travel policy
  6. How to measure quality
  7. Building the eval set
  8. When nothing relevant is retrieved the assistant makes up an answer
  9. The long system prompt and tool definitions are paid for on every question
  10. Confidential HR documents

10Confidential HR documentsFilter by the user's permissions at retrieval, before any content reaches the modelTell the model not to reveal HR content; trust users not to ask; a separate assistant nobody governsD3 Authentication and authorization gapsLesson 1, section 3.2

Scoring: 8 or more is a pass. The two principles: match retrieval to the data's shape and the query's pattern, and put only what's needed in context, and measure retrieval and answer quality separately, and fix freshness and access in the index, not the prompt.
