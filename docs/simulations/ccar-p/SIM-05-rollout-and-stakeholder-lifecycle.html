CCAR-P SIM-05 — Rollout and Stakeholder Lifecycle

How to use this: read the scene, then work through the decisions on paper, or paste the scene and the decisions list (not the answer key) into Claude and ask it to run the simulation one decision at a time with no hints. Open the coach key only when you've finished.

Primary domains: 6 Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management, 7 Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement, 4 Evaluation, Testing & Optimization · Decisions: 10 · Time: 20 min

Scene (original, written from the official objectives)

You're the lead architect for a contract-review assistant at a manufacturing company. It flags risky clauses in supplier contracts for the procurement team. Before discovery even started, the procurement VP told the executive board it would be "95% accurate". Legal, IT security and finance all have a stake, and an internal engineering team will own and run the system after you hand it over.

The decisions (answer these before you open the key)

  1. How to start
  2. Legal wants the most capable model with extended thinking on every clause; finance wants low cost and speed
  3. The eval shows 88% on clause flagging against the promised 95%
  4. Most errors are in indemnity clauses
  5. What to hand the engineering team
  6. Setting up Claude Code for that team
  7. The team loses days chasing prompt regressions
  8. What to monitor after launch
  9. After a model version change, output format shifts and a downstream report breaks
  10. Procurement users keep flagging false positives

Scoring: 8 or more is a pass. The two principles: bring bad news early, with data and options, and leave the team able to run it: documented decisions, runbooks, shared config and evals in CI.
