On your own: write your answers to a scenario's decisions, then open its coach key. Each scenario has 10 or 11 decisions, and every answer in the key cites an official task statement. In the keys, a tag like D1 1.4 means Domain 1, task statement 1.4 of the official exam guide. After the key, each scenario has a Round 2: twists section: the same scenario with one thing changed, so you practise how the right answer moves.

Readiness check: Use unseen scenario questions, explain your decisions and alternatives, and revisit errors after a delay. Local percentages and mock results are study signals; this site's mock scores do not predict an official scaled score or guarantee a pass.

Scenario learning studio connects 30 original decision variants, industry transfer, drawings and targeted questions. Practise both the six blueprint families and changed constraints.
