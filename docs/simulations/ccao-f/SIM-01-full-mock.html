CCAO-F SIM-01 — Full mock exams

Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

Take these in the order below, on different days. Exam-day conditions: one sitting, timed, no notes, no documentation, no Claude.

What counts toward being ready: only scores on questions you haven't seen before. Repeat runs are for pacing and timing, because scores rise from memory faster than skill. The full readiness bar is in Before you book.

RunMockLengthCounts toward the bar?Notes
AAmey Thakur mock 1 (Tier 2, MIT licence)15 questions, 30 minNo, it's the baselineThe cold baseline in lesson Lesson 0: Orientation: how this exam thinks. Take it before Lesson 1: Prompting and Task Execution, with no study.
BAmey Thakur mocks 2 and 3, back to back30 questions, 60 minYes, once, on your first trySave these until after the lessons. Aim for 85% or more.
CPractice tool, CCAO-F Start mock60 questions, 120 minOnly if most questions are new to youFull length, with a scaled score and a breakdown by domain. It repeats questions across runs, so later runs are pacing practice.
DPractice tool, Start mock again60 questions, 120 minPacing onlyA final timing check 1–2 days before the exam. Never on exam day.

After each run

  1. In the tool, choose Fix my mistakes to redo only the questions you got wrong.
  2. For any domain below 70%, reread that lesson's "Traps", then drill that domain in the tool.
  3. Note the date, run, score, the two weakest domains, and what you'll do next.

Note: most of the practice tool's questions are community-written, not Anthropic's. None are real exam questions. It's good for volume, but trust the official guide wherever the two disagree.
