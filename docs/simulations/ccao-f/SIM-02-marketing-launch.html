CCAO-F SIM-02 — Marketing launch brief

How to use this: read the scene, then work through the decisions on paper, or paste the scene and the decisions list (not the answer key) into Claude and ask it to run the simulation one decision at a time with no hints. Open the coach key only when you've finished.

Domains: 3 Product and Model Selection (4), 2 Output Evaluation (2), 1 Prompting (2) · Decisions: 8 · Pass: 7 of 8 · Time: 16 min

Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

Scene (read it yourself, or have Claude read it)

You're a marketing manager at a mid-size software company. In 3 weeks you launch a new analytics add-on. You have to produce a competitive landscape, a launch messaging brief, 20 short social-post variants, and a one-page summary for the VP of Marketing. The VP will act on that summary directly, and it has to weigh three analyst reports on the market that disagree with each other. The team uses Claude on a Team plan with web search, Research, Projects, Artifacts, Code Execution and a Google Drive connector. The brand voice guide and last year's launch brief are in Drive.

The decisions (answer these before you open the key)

  1. Setup: Where to do 3 weeks of launch work
  2. Competitors: Competitor moves in the last 90 days, with sources
  3. Check: The report says a competitor "cut prices 30% in August". You open the source: it says 30% off one plan, in one region, for a limited time.
  4. Brief: Weak prompt: "write launch messaging"
  5. Social posts: 20 short, varied posts
  6. Model: The VP summary must weigh three analyst reports that disagree, and the VP acts on it directly
  7. Format: A standalone brief you'll revise several times and share for comment
  8. Drift: After 50 turns, the posts ignore the voice guide
