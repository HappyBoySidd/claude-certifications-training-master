Study plan

Last reviewed: 2026-09-29

Start here: L00 orientation (30 min), then the cold baseline mock (30 min). What others learned: Insights

Who it's for. In the exam guide's own words (official wording, exam guide v1.0, section 1 and section 3): "It is intended for professionals who use Claude as a productivity tool in roles such as operations, marketing, project management, education, and communications." "The audience includes both internal staff who maintain and optimize ongoing AI-enabled workflows and external consultants who support implementation, use-case identification, and process redesign." It is not for software developers who build against APIs or design agentic systems.

For partners: it doesn't count toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility (Partner FAQ).

Before you book

Book a date early so you commit, then confirm you're ready before the reschedule window closes. The guide lets you cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment; changes inside 24 hours forfeit the fee. Play it safe and decide 48 hours out (Pearson's own page says 48).

Booking steps

  1. Sign in to the Partner Academy with your Salesforce email, directly, not "Sign in with Google" (field reports, F).
  2. Make sure your registration name matches your government photo ID exactly. To fix a name, email certifications-support@anthropic.com before you schedule (exam guide section 11, Tier 1).
  3. Check the price at checkout. If you'll be charged, get expense or training pre-approval before you pay (field reports, F).
  4. Testing online? Run the OnVUE system test on the same laptop a few days ahead. Company-managed laptops have blocked it for some people (contested, F). If the test fails, book a Pearson test center.

Ready to sit when all three are true (this is the one readiness bar for CCAO-F; other pages link here. It's our bar, not Anthropic's; you can set a stricter one):

  1. Scenario simulations: Simulation 2: Marketing launch brief to Simulation 5: Client market brief each at 7 of 8 or better, and Simulation 6: Support reply prompts at 8 of 10 or better, on your first run of each.
  2. Two timed mocks at 850 or more, with no domain under 70%, on questions you haven't seen before. Repeat runs are for pacing and don't count, because scores rise from memory. If a mock gives only a raw percentage, read 85% as 850 (our rough equivalent, not Anthropic's).
    • On the site, the unseen questions are Amey Thakur's mocks 2 and 3 (mock 1 is your cold baseline). They're 15 questions each, so take them as one 30-question, 60-minute check and count it once. That's only one unseen check on the site today; a bigger public question bank is planned for the second.
  3. Official practice exam: none exists right now. Anthropic's Partner FAQ says the practice exam on the old platform "was retired in the move to Pearson" and points to the guide's sample questions instead (Tier 1). So this line is just: get all 3 sample questions in guide section 8 right and be able to say why each wrong option is wrong.

Some study notes say to aim for 900 on practice mocks. Treat that as a stretch target; the bar above is the one we use.

Week plan (about 18 hours, about 2 weeks)

Each lesson is followed by its drill (about 15 minutes). Mocks get their own sessions and never go on exam day.

SessionTimeDo
12 hLesson 0: Orientation: how this exam thinks (30 min). Cold baseline: Amey mock 1, timed (30 min). Log the score. Read the guide's blueprint table (30 min).
22.5 hLesson 1: Prompting and Task Execution and Lesson 3: Product and Model Selection, each with its drill
32 hLesson 2: Output Evaluation and Validation (90 min), then its drill twice
42.5 hLesson 6: Governance, Risk, and Responsible Use and Lesson 4: Workflow Integration and Solution Design, each with its drill. Add the prep-path modules for any domain that's still weak.
52 hLesson 5: Configuration and Knowledge Management and Lesson 7: Troubleshooting and Optimization, each with its drill
62 hLab 01: A Claude Project that does one job well (60–90 min): set up a real Project and mark 5 outputs pass, revise or fail. Then Simulation 2: Marketing launch brief.
72.5 hSimulation 3: HR engagement survey, Simulation 4: Monthly ops report Project, Simulation 5: Client market brief and Simulation 6: Support reply prompts. Reread the traps for every miss. Drill your weakest domain, then "Fix my mistakes".
82 hTwo timed mocks on unseen questions (see Simulation 1: Full mock exams). Reread the traps in each weak lesson. Check the bar in "Before you book".

Sit the exam on a later day, rested.
