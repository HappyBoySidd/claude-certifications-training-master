How to use: read a lesson, do its "Try it" lab step, then take its practice session and log the score in your own notes.

Order: Lesson 0: Orientation: how the Developer exam thinks, then Lesson 1: Applications and Integration first (a third of the exam), then Lesson 2: Model Selection and Optimization to Lesson 7: Claude Code, Eval and Debugging in order. Don't skip Lesson 7: Claude Code, Eval and Debugging: people who passed saw more Claude Code than its 3% weight. After that, do the simulations.

Overlap with CCAR-F: about two-thirds of this exam overlaps CCAR-F (Single report: one colleague who passed, August 2026, in Insights), so take it soon after. Each lesson links the matching CCAR-F section and spends its depth on what's new for developers. Review these first:

Build as you learn. The guide asks you to build at least one app that uses the API, a tool, prompt and context engineering, and simple security and evals. Labs 02, 03 and 04 in Labs cover that.
