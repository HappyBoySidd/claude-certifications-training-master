How to use

  1. Read the lesson, then do its "Try it" exercise.
  2. Drill that domain in the practice tool without peeking; aim for 80% or more.
  3. Note the result.

Recommended order

Lesson 0: Orientation: how the Professional exam thinks first, then the biggest domains: Lesson 1: Integration Integration (19%), Lesson 2: Solution Design and Architecture Solution Design (17%), Lesson 3: Evaluation, Testing and Optimization Evaluation (16%), then Lesson 4: Governance, Safety and Risk Management through Lesson 7: Developer Productivity and Enablement. Governance and stakeholder communication together are 28% of the exam, so don't skim Lesson 4: Governance, Safety and Risk Management and Lesson 5: Stakeholder Communication and Lifecycle. After the lessons, do the simulations.

Before you start

CCAR-F is recommended but not required. Several lessons here link back to it rather than re-teaching. If you haven't done it, skim the CCAR-F lessons first.

Source: official exam guide v1.0 (sections 4–6); Insights from people who passed (CCAR-F not required, confirmed).
