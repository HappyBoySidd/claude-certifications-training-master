Your learning route: Start with one application you can picture, then choose a complete situation. Each taught layer shows a failure, asks a technical original exam-style question first, then explains the repair simply after you choose. Use this directory for its particular depth, not as another mandatory course. Return to the complete learning journey.

Build the concepts in order: Six connected coaching lessons explain the application, data flow and technical alternatives before deeper lessons and practice. Reading mode needs no setup.

New to building applications? Start with the architecture apprenticeship: eight complete original application cases, beginning with a plain-language primer, then decisions, diagrams, lab experiments, simulations, questions and conversation prompts. These are additional teaching scenarios, not additional official exam families.

Learn through scenarios and decisions

Open the Scenario learning studio for all 30 worked decision variants, matching questions, drawings, recall and five industry transfer exercises. Keep the six blueprint scenarios as your foundation, then change constraints rather than memorizing their wording.

Learn by objective

Use the 30-objective curriculum and objective lessons for full knowledge-and-skills coverage, then the industry transfer cases.

Practice changed constraints

Use the decision atlas to compare the base choice, changed constraint and failure path. Finance changes approval, monetary consistency and source-age risk; Legal changes evidence attribution, privilege/access and ambiguity; Marketing changes consent, segment criteria and false-positive cost. These are synthetic transfer contexts, not official industry weightings. Keep the underlying Claude mechanism explicit.
