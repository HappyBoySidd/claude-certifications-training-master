Insights from people who passed

A summary of every record in debriefs, by exam. The official exam guide always wins. These insights only change emphasis: hours, order, and what to practice more.

Evidence levels

  • Confirmed: reported independently by 2 or more people. It goes into lessons and curriculum as "What people who passed say".
  • Single report: one person. Shown with their name, and not treated as a rule.
  • Contested: people disagree, or it conflicts with our plan or the official guide. Both views are shown, with our call.
  • Relayed: a colleague passing on what Anthropic told them (tier F-relayed). It is credited to the colleague, never counted as Confirmed, and never shown as Anthropic's own words unless an Anthropic page (Tier 1) says the same and is linked.

Every row names the exam each report came from. Rows under "Across all exams" have reports from more than one exam, or apply to all of them.

Last updated 2026-10-03 · 18 debriefs from 12 people (one anonymous, by DM), plus posts in the #claude-certified-architect-exam-prep channel and other Salesforce Slack channels and one Slackbot Research harvest (anonymized, so its points are single reports unless they match a named record).

Order of exams

Contested.

  • Our default: CCAO-F first, as a cheap way to learn the question style.
  • A colleague, who passed all four: start with CCAR-F, because "everything else builds on it".
  • Relayed: Anthropic's advice to the Salesforce accelerated cohort (relayed by a colleague, 2026-09-24): architects should take CCAR-F and CCAR-P, and developers (FDEs) the Developer track. "Pursue a Foundations certification where possible, preferably CCAR-F." CCAR-F and CCAR-P "can be pursued independently; neither is a prerequisite for the other."
  • A colleague: CCAO-F is "the easiest to crack out of all 4".
  • Decided 2026-09-27: CCAO-F first only for people who don't write code. Architects and developers start with CCAR-F; architects take CCAR-P next and developers CCDV-F. The full paths are in the curriculum overview.
